INVESTOR SAFETY

Protect Yourself First

01

Never Share OTP

Never disclose OTPs, PINs, passwords or banking credentials to unexpected contacts.

02

Question Guaranteed Returns

Promises of guaranteed or unusually high returns should trigger additional verification.

03

Don't Rush

Urgency can prevent you from taking enough time to verify a financial communication.

04

Verify Independently

Find official websites and contact details independently instead of trusting supplied links.

05

Keep Evidence

Preserve suspicious messages, screenshots, payment records and other evidence.

06

Verify Before Paying

Don't send money simply because a message claims a fee is required.

NiveshRaksha AI is an educational investor-safety prototype designed to identify common warning signs in suspicious financial communications.

It does not recommend investments. Instead, it helps users pause, identify warning signs and verify information independently.

Risk The possibility that an investment outcome differs from what was expected.
Diversification Spreading investments rather than depending on one asset or opportunity.
Verification Independently checking whether a person, organization or claim is genuine.
